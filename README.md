# Seattle Airbnb Market Analysis
A Tableau dashboard analyzing Airbnb listing prices and revenue trends in Seattle (2016), by bedroom count and zipcode.

##  What's Inside

- **Revenue for Year:** weekly revenue trend across 2016
- **Average Price per Bedroom:** nightly price for 1–6 bedroom listings
- **Distinct Count of Bedroom Listings:** supply by bedroom count
- **Price by Zipcode:** ranked average price per zipcode
- **Price per Zipcode (Map):** geographic view of prices

##  Key Insights

- **Bigger = pricier:** average price rises from **$96.2** (1 bed) to **$584.8** (6 beds).
- **Small units dominate:** 1–2 bedroom listings make up about **89%** of all listings (1,811 + 483 of ~2,580).
- **Location matters:** zipcode **98134** is the most expensive (~$206.6), while **98125** is the cheapest (~$64.7), about a 3x gap.
- **Revenue trend:** weekly revenue grows from ~$1M in January to ~$2M by late May, then stays steady through December.

##  Note

5- and 6-bedroom categories have very few listings (20 and 5), so their averages are less reliable.

##  Tools
